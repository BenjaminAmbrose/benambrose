Who am I?

I'm a biophysicist and Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Rueda Lab at the MRC Laboratory of Medical Sciences (LMS) in London. I use single-molecule methods to watch biological machines at work. That includes smFRET, TIRF microscopy, FCS and correlative optical tweezers with fluorescence. At the moment I'm working on how chromatin remodellers such as INO80 move and position nucleosomes along DNA.

I also build instruments and software. During my PhD in the Craggs Lab at the University of Sheffield, I developed the smfBox, an open-source smFRET microscope. I have since co-developed a commercial smFRET instrument and written analysis tools such as KymoSketch. I care about open science and making advanced microscopy accessible to everyone. In my spare time I like to make animations of biomolecules in motion. I also chair the MRC LMS Postdoctoral Committee.

Publications

INO80 rapidly shuttles nucleosomes between chromatin barriers

bioRxiv · 2026 · in review at Molecular Cell
Ambrose B, Girvan P, Skehan MT, McCormack EA, Egharevba DI, Rueda DS

Using correlative optical tweezers, fluorescence microscopy and smFRET, we show that human INO80 diffuses along DNA and, on meeting a nucleosome, slides it rapidly over thousands of base pairs in bidirectional bursts. Neighbouring nucleosomes act as barriers. They confine the mobile nucleosome to shuttling back and forth between chromatin boundaries, and two INO80 complexes can occupy a single nucleosome at the same time.

DOI: 10.64898/2026.09.20.752975

The smfBox is an open-source platform for single-molecule FRET

Nature Communications · 2020
Ambrose B, Baxter JM, Cully J, Willmott M, Steele EM, Bateman BC, Martin-Fernandez ML, Cadby A, Shewring J, Aaldering M, Craggs TD

Single-molecule Förster Resonance Energy Transfer (smFRET) is a powerful technique capable of resolving both relative and absolute distances within and between structurally dynamic biomolecules. High instrument costs, and a lack of open-source hardware and acquisition software have limited smFRET's broad application by non-specialists. Here, we present the smfBox, a cost-effective confocal smFRET platform, providing detailed build instructions, open-source acquisition software, and full validation, thereby democratising smFRET for the wider scientific community.

DOI: 10.1038/s41467-020-19468-4

The transitional kinetics between open and closed Rep structures can be tuned by salt via two intermediate states

Nucleic Acids Research · 2026
Howard JAL, Ambrose B, Abdelhamid MAS, et al.

Combining TCSPC, FCS, rapid smFRET, ABEL trapping and molecular dynamics, we resolve two previously hidden intermediate states between the open and closed structures of the DNA helicase Rep, whose stability is tuned by salt.

DOI: 10.1093/nar/gkaf1483

A new twist on PIFE: photoisomerisation-related fluorescence enhancement

Methods and Applications in Fluorescence · 2024
Ploetz E, Ambrose B, Barth A, Börner R, et al.

A community review proposing that PIFE be renamed after its underlying mechanism, photoisomerisation-related fluorescence enhancement. It covers cyanine photochemistry, quantitative PIFE assays, and current and future applications.
