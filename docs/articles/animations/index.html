INO80 on a nucleosome

An animation of the chromatin remodeller INO80 on a nucleosome.

Related paper: Ambrose B, et al. INO80 rapidly shuttles nucleosomes between chromatin barriers. bioRxiv (2026). DOI: 10.64898/2026.09.20.752975

Rep helicase with FRET dyes

An animation of the DNA helicase Rep, labelled with a pair of FRET dyes. Rep switches between open and closed conformations, which changes the distance between the dyes. That changes the FRET efficiency, and so how brightly each dye shines.

Related paper: Howard JAL, Ambrose B, et al. The transitional kinetics between open and closed Rep structures can be tuned by salt via two intermediate states. Nucleic Acids Research (2026). DOI: 10.1093/nar/gkaf1483

SWR1 nucleosome flipping

An animation of the chromatin remodeller SWR1 and nucleosome flipping.

Related paper: Girvan P, et al. Nucleosome flipping drives kinetic proofreading and processivity by SWR1. Nature (2024). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-08152-y

CRISPR–Cas9 on a DNA minicircle

An animation of CRISPR–Cas9 on a DNA minicircle.

Related paper: Smith, et al. Structural basis of supercoiling-induced CRISPR–Cas9 off-target activity. Nature (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10255-7

ANKLE1 endonuclease

An animation of the ultrafine-bridge-associated endonuclease ANKLE1 on DNA.

Related paper: Ray, et al. The ultrafine-bridge-associated endonuclease ANKLE1 is stimulated by tension in DNA to process branch-points. Nucleic Acids Research (2026). DOI: 10.1093/nar/gkag585
