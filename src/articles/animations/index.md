### Rep helicase with FRET dyes

<video controls loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" src="../../moleculeanims/repvidwithglo0001-0600.mp4"></video>

An animation of the DNA helicase Rep, labelled with a pair of FRET dyes. Rep switches between open and closed conformations, which changes the distance between the dyes. That changes the FRET efficiency, and so how brightly each dye shines.

Related paper: Howard JAL, Ambrose B, et al. *The transitional kinetics between open and closed Rep structures can be tuned by salt via two intermediate states.* Nucleic Acids Research (2026). [DOI: 10.1093/nar/gkaf1483](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf1483)
